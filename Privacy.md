# LPD Officer Monitor Privacy Policy
**Last updated:** August 6, 2026

## 1. About this policy

LPD Officer Monitor (the **Application**) is operated by the **LPD Community**, a fictional police roleplay community based in Texas, United States. The Application helps administer the LPD Community's Discord server and LPD-owned VRChat worlds.

This policy explains what information the Application accesses, collects, uses, stores, discloses, and deletes. It applies to information processed through the Application and does not replace the separate privacy policies of Discord, VRChat, GitHub, OVHcloud, or other third-party services.

By participating in the LPD Community, members may interact with features that require certain information. Members who do not want the Application to process the content of a leave-of-absence request may contact LPD staff directly instead of submitting the request in a monitored channel.

## 2. Who may use the Application

The LPD Community and the Application are intended only for people who are at least 16 years old and who meet Discord's minimum age requirement for their country. They are not directed to children under 13.

If we learn that the Application has collected personal information from someone who does not meet these age requirements, we will take appropriate steps to remove the information. A parent or guardian may contact LPD staff as described in [Section 11](#11-contact-and-privacy-requests).

## 3. Information we process

Depending on the features a member uses, the Application may process the following categories of information.

### Discord membership and account information

- Discord user IDs;
- Discord usernames and display names when needed for operation, staff output, or logs;
- server, channel, message, and role IDs;
- current Discord roles and changes to those roles;
- membership status, rank, team membership, training, certifications, badges, and qualifications; and
- the date monitoring began and the date and reason a membership record was deactivated.

Persistent membership records are created for members who join the LPD roleplay group and receive a qualifying Discord role. The Application may temporarily process information about other server participants when performing security or anti-scam functions.

### VRChat information

- VRChat usernames and user IDs voluntarily registered by members;
- VRChat group registration and invitation status; and
- information needed to invite eligible members to the LPD VRChat group and grant access to LPD-owned VRChat worlds and events.

### Attendance and community activity

- participation in designated Discord voice channels;
- patrol, event, call, and voice-session start and end times;
- associated voice-channel names and IDs;
- event-host and squad information; and
- virtual roleplay-credit records earned through patrol participation and used within the LPD Community.

These roleplay credits are a non-monetary, in-community game mechanic. They have no cash value, cannot be purchased, sold, exchanged, or redeemed for money, and are not financial-account or payment-card information. The Application records participation and timing information but does not record the audio content of voice conversations.

### Leave-of-absence information

- the member's Discord user ID;
- the submitted start and end dates;
- the reason supplied by the member;
- the Discord message and channel IDs; and
- creation, update, and deactivation timestamps.

Leave-of-absence reasons are supplied voluntarily in a designated, role-restricted channel. They may contain personal information chosen by the member. Members should not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for staff to evaluate the request.

### Moderation and security information

- strike records, including the reason, timestamp, affected member, and submitting staff member;
- detention status and the Discord roles that must be restored later;
- member join, departure, and relevant role-change information; and
- messages or links processed to detect suspected scam activity and provide bot-command guidance.

Suspicious-link message content is processed for the security of the Discord server and may be held temporarily while the Application determines whether related messages require moderation. Leave-of-absence reasons are stored as described above. The Application does not generally archive all Discord messages.

### Technical information and logs

- command or interaction names, Discord user and channel IDs, timestamps, and error details;
- operational and security events;
- role and membership changes relevant to Application functions; and
- diagnostic information generated when the Application or a third-party API fails.

Error logs may be transmitted to private Discord channels or webhooks accessible only to authorized LPD staff and members of the LPD Programming Team.

## 4. How we use information

The LPD Community uses information processed by the Application to:

- onboard members and deactivate records when members leave or lose a qualifying role;
- assign and track ranks, roles, training, badges, teams, and qualifications;
- associate Discord members with their VRChat accounts;
- invite eligible members to the LPD VRChat group;
- generate the allowlist and access attributes used by LPD-owned VRChat worlds;
- record patrol, event, call, and voice-channel attendance;
- recognize approved leave periods when evaluating member activity;
- administer community moderation and restore roles after detention;
- detect suspected scam links and help users correct unsuccessful bot commands;
- secure, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Application; and
- comply with applicable legal requirements and Discord's platform rules.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use message content or other personal information to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

## 5. Message content and member choice

The Application accesses message content only for limited community-management, command-support, and security functions. In particular, it parses leave-of-absence submissions in a designated, role-restricted channel, provides guidance in designated bot channels, and checks messages available to it for suspected scam links.

Submitting a leave-of-absence request through the monitored channel is voluntary. A member who does not want the Application to process the request's message content may contact an LPD staff member directly through Discord. Choosing not to provide information required for a feature may prevent the member from using that automated feature.

## 6. Public VRChat-world allowlist

The Application publishes an allowlist file through a public GitHub Gist so that LPD-owned VRChat worlds can determine member access and capabilities. Anyone with access to the public Gist may view this information.

The published file may include:

- VRChat username;
- LPD rank and staff status;
- training and certification status;
- team, event-host, moderator, recruiter, mentor, supporter, approver, and similar role-status flags;
- language-role flags selected or held in the LPD Discord server; and
- virtual roleplay-credit values earned through patrol participation and related timestamps used by LPD worlds.

Discord user IDs, leave-of-absence reasons, message content, voice-session history, strike reasons, and VRChat invite tokens are not intended to be included in the public allowlist.

Publication is limited to information required for the proper functioning of LPD-owned VRChat worlds. Removing information required by the allowlist may also remove or limit the member's access to those worlds and related events.

## 7. How information is disclosed and stored

Except for the public allowlist described in [Section 6](#6-public-vrchat-world-allowlist), stored information is available only to people and service providers who need it for the purposes described in this policy.

- **LPD Programming Team:** Members of the LPD Programming Team may access the Application's stored information and private operational or error logs to operate, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot it.
- **LPD staff:** Authorized staff may view operational or error information sent to private Discord channels. Staff receive privacy requests and refer requests requiring access to stored Application data to the LPD Programming Team.
- **Discord:** The Application receives information through Discord's APIs and sends commands, responses, moderation notices, and private operational logs through Discord.
- **OVHcloud:** The Application and its database are hosted in a United States OVHcloud data center.
- **GitHub:** The public allowlist is hosted in a GitHub Gist so LPD-owned VRChat worlds can retrieve it.
- **VRChat:** VRChat usernames and user IDs are used with the VRChat API to find members and invite eligible members to the LPD VRChat group.

We do not otherwise provide personal information outside the LPD Community except at a member's direction, to service providers needed to operate the Application, when the public allowlist requires it, or when required by law or necessary to protect the rights and safety of users, the LPD Community, or others.

Because the LPD Community accepts members worldwide and its providers operate internationally, information may be processed in the United States or other countries whose privacy laws may differ from those in the member's location.

## 8. Retention

The LPD Community has not adopted one fixed retention period for every category of information. We retain information only for as long as it remains reasonably necessary for the community-management, access-control, attendance, moderation, security, and operational purposes described in this policy.

In general:

- active membership, VRChat, rank, training, qualification, and role information is retained while needed to administer membership and access;
- attendance, patrol, event, virtual roleplay-credit, and related historical records are retained while needed for activity administration and community records;
- leave-of-absence dates and reasons are retained while needed to administer leave and related activity decisions;
- moderation and security records are retained while needed to enforce community rules, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and protect the community;
- technical logs are retained according to operational needs and storage limits; and
- information in infrastructure backups may remain until the relevant backup expires, for no longer than one year.

When a member leaves or loses a qualifying role, the Application deactivates the member's record and removes the member from the active public allowlist. Some historical information may remain where it is still needed for the purposes above. We will delete or anonymize personal information when it is no longer necessary, when Discord requests its deletion, when the Application ceases operation, or following a verified user request, except where continued retention is required by law.

Backups are maintained for disaster recovery. When information is deleted from active systems, residual copies may remain in backups until those backups expire. If a backup is restored, deletion requests applicable to restored information will be reapplied where reasonably feasible.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include restricting access to authorized personnel, protecting credentials, encrypting the production database at rest, and using encrypted connections when supported for information transmitted between the Application and its providers.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. If we discover an incident involving Application data, we will investigate and notify affected users, Discord, or authorities when required.

## 10. Privacy rights

Depending on where a member lives, they may have rights concerning their personal information. These may include rights to:

- request access to information associated with them;
- ask us to correct inaccurate information;
- request deletion of information;
- object to or restrict certain processing; and
- obtain a portable copy of certain information they provided.

We will evaluate and respond to verified requests as required by applicable law and Discord's terms. We will not discriminate against a member for exercising an applicable privacy right. However, deleting information necessary for membership, the public allowlist, or another feature may prevent continued access to that feature.

If we cannot fulfill a request completely, we will explain the reason when required. Information may be retained where reasonably necessary to comply with law, maintain security, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, or establish or defend legal claims.

## 11. Contact and privacy requests

Members may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their Application data by sending a direct message on Discord to an LPD staff member who has the designated staff role in the LPD Community server.

The request should identify that it concerns **LPD Officer Monitor privacy data** and describe the requested action. Staff may ask the requester to verify control of the Discord account associated with the information. Requests will be processed within a reasonable period and within any period required by applicable law.

Members who are no longer in the LPD Community server may contact an LPD staff member with whom they previously communicated on Discord. A parent or guardian making a request concerning a minor should provide enough information for staff to identify the relevant account and verify the requester's authority.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Application, its providers, or its data practices change. The latest version will show the effective date and last-updated date at the beginning of the document. When reasonably practical, material changes will also be announced through the LPD Community's Discord server.
